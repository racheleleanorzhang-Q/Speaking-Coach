# Acceptance tests

Use these scenarios to evaluate behavior, not exact wording. A test passes when the observable outcome and constraints are satisfied.

## Core flow

1. **Unspecified practice:** “开始今天的练习。” The coach uses known context or chooses a sensible 15-minute session, states one focus, and starts within two short turns.
2. **Five-minute limit:** “我只有五分钟。” The coach compresses warm-up and practice while preserving review and retry.
3. **Frequent errors:** The learner makes many small mistakes. The coach does not interrupt repeatedly and selects no more than three issues.
4. **Mandatory transfer:** After feedback, the coach asks for a fresh spoken answer that uses the improved form.
5. **Retry declined:** The learner refuses to retry. The coach stays supportive, records the session as incomplete if tracking is active, and does not falsely mark completion.

## Adaptation

6. **Beginner in Chinese:** “我英语很差，不知道怎么说。” The coach uses concise Chinese support and elicits one manageable English sentence.
7. **Advanced learner:** The learner answers fluently and naturally. The coach avoids elementary explanations and focuses on nuance or interaction.
8. **Just chat:** “今天只想聊天，不想上课。” The coach minimizes intervention and offers only an optional short recap.
9. **Real-time correction request:** The learner explicitly asks to be corrected immediately. The coach honors that preference without correcting harmless alternatives.
10. **Unknown topic:** The learner does not choose a topic. The coach selects one instead of presenting a long menu.

## Modes

11. **Meeting roleplay:** The learner asks to practice disagreeing in a meeting. The coach defines roles briefly, stays in role, and delays the model answer until after an attempt.
12. **Review session:** The coach has two recent repeated issues. It creates prompts requiring those forms rather than merely showing the corrections again.
13. **Baseline:** The coach gathers sustained and interactive evidence, separates transcript observations from audio observations, and avoids presenting an accredited score.
14. **Weekly report with sparse data:** Only one tracked session exists. The coach reports that limitation and does not invent trends.

## Persistence and truthfulness

15. **Writable workspace:** A coaching data directory is in scope. The coach uses the templates, writes a concise record, and confirms the actual path.
16. **No persistence:** No writable record or memory tool is available. The coach provides a saveable summary and does not say it remembered or saved anything.
17. **Public repository:** Personal profile and session records remain ignored by Git; only templates are versioned.
18. **Transcript-only surface:** The coach does not claim to detect stress, intonation, or exact pronunciation from text alone.
