# English Speaking Practice — 2026-10-26

今天的练习包括两部分：外贸商务自我介绍，以及日常晨间生活。建议先慢速朗读一遍，再用自然语速朗读两遍。

## Part 1: Business Introduction

### Full Dialogue

**Rachel:**  
Hi, I'm Rachel. I'm based in Beijing, China. I'm exploring opportunities in international trade and global business. I'd like to improve my spoken English for communicating with overseas clients. My goal is to speak clearly, confidently, and professionally.

**Client:**  
Nice to meet you. What kind of products or industry are you interested in?

**Rachel:**  
Nice to meet you, too. I'm focusing on pet products, especially for dogs and cats, such as clothes and toys. We target pet owners who care about quality and style.

**Client:**  
That sounds interesting. What makes your products better than others?

**Rachel:**  
Our products focus on quality and customization. We offer tailored designs for different pets.

**Client:**  
That sounds great. I'd like to learn more about your products.

**Rachel:**  
Thank you. I'd be happy to introduce our products and discuss how we can work together.

### Continuous Reading Version

Hi, I'm Rachel. I'm based in Beijing, China. I'm exploring opportunities in international trade and global business. I'd like to improve my spoken English for communicating with overseas clients. My goal is to speak clearly, confidently, and professionally.

I'm focusing on pet products, especially for dogs and cats, such as clothes and toys. We target pet owners who care about quality and style. Our products focus on quality and customization. We offer tailored designs for different pets.

Thank you. I'd be happy to introduce our products and discuss how we can work together.

### Key Expressions

- **be based in Beijing, China** — 位于中国北京
- **explore opportunities in international trade** — 探索国际贸易机会
- **communicate with overseas clients** — 与海外客户沟通
- **focus on pet products** — 专注于宠物产品
- **care about quality and style** — 注重品质和风格
- **quality and customization** — 品质与定制化
- **tailored designs** — 定制设计
- **discuss how we can work together** — 讨论合作方式

## Part 2: My Morning Routine

### Natural Reading Version

In the morning, when I wake up, I wash my face and put on some light makeup. After that, I have breakfast, usually an egg and a cup of coffee. Once I'm ready, I head to work. On my way to work, I sometimes listen to music or an English podcast. I drive to the office, and it takes about ten to fifteen minutes.

### Sentence-by-Sentence Practice

1. In the morning, when I wake up, I wash my face and put on some light makeup.
2. After that, I have breakfast, usually an egg and a cup of coffee.
3. Once I'm ready, I head to work.
4. On my way to work, I sometimes listen to music or an English podcast.
5. I drive to the office, and it takes about ten to fifteen minutes.

### Natural Transitions

- **After that, ...** — 然后；在那之后
- **Once I'm ready, ...** — 等我准备好以后
- **On my way to work, ...** — 在上班路上
- **usually ...** — 通常……
- **it takes about ...** — 大约需要……时间

## Suggested Reading Routine

1. 慢速朗读全文，确保每个词读清楚。
2. 按意群朗读，不要逐词停顿。
3. 第二遍加入自然停顿和语调。
4. 第三遍不看中文，尽量像真实聊天一样表达。
5. 最后尝试不看文本，用自己的话复述两段内容。

## Today's Corrections

- Say **I'm based in Beijing, China**, not “I'm based in Beijing in China.”
- Say **wash my face**, not “clear my face.”
- Say **put on some light makeup**, not “do some simple makeup.”
- Say **I drive to the office**, not “I drive my car to my company.”
- Use **offer** for a current business capability: “We offer tailored designs.”
- Use **our products** in a business introduction when speaking for a company.

## Final Sentence of the Day
